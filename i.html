about

the art, the artist

i'm ameya — a 20-year-old henna artist, uiuc student, and deloitte intern who started mehndi by ameya on 10.19.2025. i do this because i love it, and because this art form deserves to be treated with the respect it's always had.

mehndi and jagua have deep roots in south asian, middle eastern, and north african cultures. i take that seriously — whether i'm doing a full bridal set or a tattoo-style custom piece for a college student who's never had henna before.
