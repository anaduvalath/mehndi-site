For bridal appointments, I recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks in advance. For parties and individual sessions, 1–2 weeks is usually sufficient, though earlier is always better to secure your preferred date.
Mehndi typically lasts 1–3 weeks depending on skin type, placement, and aftercare. Hands and feet tend to stain darkest and last longest. Following aftercare instructions closely makes a big difference.
Avoid applying lotion or oil to the area on the day of your appointment. Exfoliating the skin a day before can also help the henna absorb more deeply for a richer stain.
